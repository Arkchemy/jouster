2026-08-29 — what the real game's registration list looks like when igFile runs
================================================================================

Build 22:28 established that igFile is never dispatched to at any level
(igfile mask=0x38: the control's whole chain runs, igFile's none of it). So
the question became whether the driver ever sees it. Cemu answers that from
the working side.

BREAKING ON arkRegister(igFile) IN THE RETAIL GAME
---------------------------------------------------
    lr  = 0x215b9fc
    r25 = 0x10 (16)   r26 = 0x11dd9680   r27 = 0x15 (21)   r29 = 0x24 (36)

r14..r31 are non-volatile on PowerPC, so at the callee's entry the driver's own
registers are still intact -- which is what makes this readable without a stack
walk.

    list header at r26 : array=0x359e0b7c  count=0x15 (21)  capacity=0x1c (28)

    entry array:
      0x021bc3d8  0x021bc4a8  0x021b3f24  0x021b8664
      0x021c0ad4  0x021c06b4  0x021c1348  0x021af868
      0x021b8038  0x021b825c  0x021b95ec  0x021b1594
      0x021ca8d8  0x021b2eb4  0x021c043c  0x021c050c
                              ^^^^^^^^^^ igFile, index 13

So the list igFile belongs to holds 21 entries and igFile is number 13.

WHICH DISPATCH SITE, AND WHY IT MATTERS
---------------------------------------
getClassMetaSafeInternal walks the list unrolled eight at a time, then handles
count & 7 in a remainder tail. With 21 entries that is two full iterations of
eight, then five in the remainder.

lr = 0x215b9fc is the fifth bctrl of the main loop, and index 13 is 8 + 5 --
the second iteration. So igFile is dispatched by the MAIN LOOP, not the
remainder.

Our own control, igStorageDevice, arrived from 0x215ba4c, the remainder. That
looked like evidence the main loop never runs, and it is not: a class whose
list has fewer than eight entries is handled entirely by the remainder, which
is ordinary. That inference was made and then dropped.

Also checked and cleared while here: rlwinm. at 0x215b978 computes count/8 and
sets CR0, and if that CR0 update were missing the ble after it would skip the
main loop entirely -- which would have explained everything. It is not
missing. All 6,912 rlwinm. instructions in the tree emit their CR0 update.
