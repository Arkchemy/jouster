WHICH JOINS THE TWO OPEN PROBLEMS INTO ONE
------------------------------------------
endArkRegister takes one class per call: index at this->0x40, count at
*(this->0x1c), and when the index catches up it branches to the drained path,
which is where userRegister is reached. Retail:

    endArkRegister      846+ calls, all from lr=0x215baa4
    userRegister        1 call

So retail drains the list once, at the end of roughly a thousand classes. Ours
reaches that same drained path six times -- and registration has been stuck at
124 classes for days.

Those have been tracked as two separate problems. They are one: a list that is
short, drained, refilled, and drained again. The pool exhaustion is the third
face of it, since each drain reconstructs the same singletons and re-allocates
the same 971KB.
