AND THE NUMBER THAT ACTUALLY MATTERS WAS NEVER BEING READ
----------------------------------------------------------
Two store watches have been armed since the 00:19 build. Only one was ever
looked at.

    watch #1  +0x18 (limit)   hits=3  changed=1  last=0x0  @call 1,610,588
    watch #2  +0x14 (cursor)  hits=4  changed=3  last=0x1  @call 1,610,796

The cursor was written FOUR times, changed THREE times, and settled at 1 --
and it did so AFTER the limit was written, 208 calls later.

Three rounds have gone into "the limit is zero and nothing sets it". The
guard is:

    2168954: cmplw r30, r4        [e+0x14] vs [e+0x18]
    2168958: bgt   skip           skip when cursor > limit

A cursor of 1 against a limit of 0 fails. A cursor of 0 against a limit of 0
PASSES -- 0 > 0 is false -- and work starts. So a limit that is correctly
zero with a cursor that should not have reached 1 explains everything
observed, exactly as well as the reading pursued so far.

Something drove that cursor through three distinct values immediately after
construction. The limit was written once and left. On the evidence, the
cursor is the more active and the less understood of the two, and it has
never had its history recorded.

WHY THE HISTORY WAS MISSING
---------------------------
Watch #1 is the one wired to the polled history buffer -- ppc_poll_watch_mem
records call, value, pc and lr for up to 8 changes. Watch #2 keeps only the
latest write. The limit sat on #1 and produced a complete story in one run;
the cursor sat on #2 and discarded two of its three changes every run.

That was an arbitrary choice made when the addresses were first armed, and
it happened to point the good instrument at the less interesting field.

THIS BUILD
----------
Swapped:

    watch #1 (with history) -> 0xf7d0600   the cursor
    watch #2 (latest only)  -> 0xf7d0604   the limit, already fully answered

    memwatch: n=%u [i]@call=value pc= lr=   for up to 8 changes

The sequence of cursor values, and who wrote each, is the measurement.
0 -> 1 written once by construction is one story; 0 -> something -> 1 driven
by a later caller is a different one, and the lr on each entry names it.
