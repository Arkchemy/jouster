Both the "published too late" story and the "0x80001 is the corruption" story
were downstream symptoms.

WHY IT LANDS THERE
------------------
allocateBucket's loop:

    2164ad4: bl 0x214ae78          allocate the bucket's buffer
    2164ae0: stw r25, 0x10(r26)    bucket->count
    2164ae8: lwz r29, 0xc(r26)     r29 = bucket->buffer
    2164af0: lwz r8, 0(r28)        vtable
    2164af4: lwz r0, 0x84(r8)      vtable+0x84 = __constructElement
    2164b00: mr r4, r29            element pointer
    2164b08: bctrl

constructElement writes through the pointer it is handed. For the write to
land on 0x34D8, bucket->0xc held a value around 0x34D0 -- not an allocation,
a small number. So the allocation at 0x214ae78 is where this begins.
