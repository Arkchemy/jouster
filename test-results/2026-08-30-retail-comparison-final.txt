and retail's buffer holds real content (0x116d9390 twice, then zeros), so it

That is the entire divergence in three registers. The metaobject is fine on
both sides. The pool is real in retail and null here, and the buffer follows
from it: a null pool yields a null allocation, element addresses get computed
from that null base, and out come small integers -- 0xc, 0x34D4, 0x47430 --
used as pointers.

WHAT REMAINS
------------
Retail reaches this call with a real pool. Ours does not, even though
igPool::activate resolves a default pool successfully 16 times out of 17 and
stores it at +0x20. So either the failing path does not go through activate's
fallback, or it reads +0x20 before the store lands.

That is one narrow question and it is the last one in this chain.

WHAT IS SETTLED
---------------
Every instruction along the way translates correctly. The refcount increment,
the flag write at buffer+4, the size-class mapping, the free-list walk, the
vtable dispatch -- all verified against the retail game and all faithful. The
failure is a single null value propagating through correct code, and the
guest-memory mask is what turns each consequence into silence rather than a
crash.
