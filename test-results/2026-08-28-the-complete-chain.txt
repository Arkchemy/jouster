A CORRECTION MADE WHILE WRITING THIS

The strlen loop at 21a6b88 was briefly recorded as a recompiler bug --
`lbzu` loading without performing its register update. It is not. The
update is emitted on the following line:

  ctx->r[12] = ppc_load_u8(ctx, ctx->r[11] + (int32_t)1);
  ctx->r[11] = ctx->r[11] + (int32_t)1;

The claim came from reading one line of a two-line translation. That is
the third time today a suspected recompiler bug turned out to be correct
code read too quickly.
