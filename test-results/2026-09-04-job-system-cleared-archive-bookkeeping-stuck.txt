THE PROBLEM WITH BOTH OF THOSE MEASUREMENTS
-------------------------------------------
Both were previously reported as "never written after init". Both were
measured with store watches that could not see stwcx., because the atomics
fix routed atomic stores around the watch hook. Both are lock-free counters
-- exactly the category that gap concealed.

So those two results, which a large amount of yesterday's reasoning rested
on, are unsound and are being re-measured with the corrected watches.

This is the second time an instrument, rather than the engine, produced the
wrong answer today. The first was sampling a microsecond-scale ring once per
frame and reading the settled state as no activity. Both were mine.
