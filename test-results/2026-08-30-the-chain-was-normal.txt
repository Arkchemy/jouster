WHAT THIS MEANS
---------------
The chain traced over the last several rounds -- null pool into activate, into
allocateBucket, into constructElement, into createInstanceInPlace -- is normal
engine behaviour, not a defect. Every step of it matches the retail game.

Re-reading our own numbers with that in mind: ctorelem reports buf=0x47430, a
valid buffer, on 24,645 calls. The null-mem case, nullinst, fired exactly ONCE
in a two-minute run. The single unrepresentative sample is what the
dispatch-miss probe happened to record first, and it set the direction for
several builds.

THE ACTUAL MISTAKE
------------------
g_ppc_dispatch_miss_* recorded only the FIRST miss. With ~74,000 of them,
describing the population from its first arrival was never sound, and here it
picked a one-off whose this=0 pointed at machinery that turns out to be
correct.
