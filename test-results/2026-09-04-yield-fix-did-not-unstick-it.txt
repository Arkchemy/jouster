A CORRECTION TO MY OWN CLAIM
----------------------------
I reported the yield fix made execution "1.9x slower", from 158 calls/frame
to 84. That was wrong, and the error was a bad comparison rather than a bad
measurement.

The 84 came from a run Aaron stopped at frame 3960, while the boot was still
in class registration -- a different and much heavier workload phase than the
steady state. Comparing it against a full run's average was not valid.

Measured properly, on two full runs:

    before the fix   3,979,347 calls / 25,140 frames = 158.3 per frame
    after the fix    3,939,494 calls / 25,140 frames = 156.7 per frame

About 1% apart, which is noise. sched_yield in these spin loops is
effectively free. The earlier figure should not be relied on.
