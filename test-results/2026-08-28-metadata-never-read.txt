address inside igRegistry's meta-object descriptor, is never read at
all. That separates the two candidates cleanly:

  - Not a dispatch-resolution bug in the recompiler. If ppc_dispatch

    still have been READ before the call was attempted. It was not.
  - It is engine ordering: the walk that consumes meta-object
    descriptors never starts.
