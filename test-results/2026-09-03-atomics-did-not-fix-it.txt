WHY IT DID NOT HELP -- THE SLEEP LOOP DOES NOT POLL THE QUEUE
-------------------------------------------------------------
    L_21da144:  sleeping_count++
                OSWaitEventWithTimeout(evt, 0, 100000000)
                sleeping_count--
    L_21da17c:  lwz r10, 0x6b20(r27)   A = [445456]
                bne exit
                lbz r11, -0x29fe(r30)  B = byte[3524]
                bne exit
                lwz r0, -0x2a1c(r29)   C = [14244]
                beq L_21da144          all zero -> sleep again

The worker never looks at the batch queue while it sleeps. It polls three
globals, and leaves the loop only when one of them is non-zero. So making
the queue's own atomics correct could not have helped: the queue is not what
is being read.

That also explains the odd counts. jqPopNextBatch sits OUTSIDE this loop, so
4 pops and 285 timed wakeups are consistent: the worker entered
jqWorkerSleep a third time and has been cycling inside it ever since,
without ever returning far enough to pop again.

The timeout constant is 100,000,000 ticks = 1.61 s against the Espresso
timebase, and 420 s / 285 wakes = 1.47 s. Those agree, which confirms the
workers really are waking on schedule and finding all three flags zero.
