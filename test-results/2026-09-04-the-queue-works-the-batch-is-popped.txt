TWO WRONG CONCLUSIONS, WITHDRAWN
--------------------------------
1. "The enqueue fails inside jqAddBatchToQueue." It does not. The tail
   advances, from that exact function.
2. "The ring is always empty -- head == tail in all 420 frames." True as
   measured and false as interpreted. The enqueue/dequeue pair completes in
   about four guest calls; a once-per-frame snapshot can only ever catch the
   settled state.

Both came from the same error: reading a once-per-frame sample of something
that moves in microseconds as evidence of no activity. The "double buffer"
misreading earlier was this same ring, sampled the same way. Three
conclusions, one bad habit.

The lesson is specific enough to be worth stating: a per-frame snapshot can
prove a value IS something, never that it is never anything else. For that,
a store watch with history is the right instrument, and it was available the
whole time.
