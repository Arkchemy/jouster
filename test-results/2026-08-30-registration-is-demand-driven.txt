drain has idx == cnt with the count climbing 6, 8, 30, 35, 36, 38, 43 ... 124.
Draining is the normal rhythm of registration, not a fault: classes are
appended, what is there is processed, more are appended. Only 6 of those 35
drains reach userRegister.

And registration stopping at 124 is not itself a bug. Retail calls
getClassMetaSafeInternal 13,567 times from many different call sites -- it is
demand-driven, the metaobject being built the first time something asks for
it. Ours asks 105 times. We do not stall because registration stops; we stop
registering because the boot stalls, and 124 is simply how far it gets. The
frontier class, #125, is igInternalLockableHandleMemoryPool, and nothing is
wrong with it -- nothing asks.

    2154f50: bge 0x215507c        nothing new -> return
    2154f54: subf r28, r27, r0    run only entries [mark, count)

The translation is faithful: cmpw reads r8 before the store, and 0x44 is
written with the count. So this is not a seventh recompiler bug, and the
depth==1 test explains why only 6 of 35 drains get this far.
