The control's whole chain runs. igFile's is never entered at any level, so
nothing inside those functions is failing -- the driver never reaches them.

WHAT THE DATA LOOKS LIKE
------------------------
Now that data relocations are applied, both classes' registration entry points
are written into .rodata by ppc_init_globals. The counts differ:

    &arkRegister(igFile)            written once   at 1152044
    &arkRegister(igStorageDevice)   written 3x     at 1135428, 1135464, 1136264

Three is explained: igPhysicalStorageDevice, igVirtualStorageDevice and
igMemoryStorageDevice all derive from igStorageDevice, so the base appears in
each subclass's table as well as its own. igFile has one entry, its own.

So igFile's entry EXISTS in data. It is simply never walked.

THIS BUILD (22:34)
------------------
The driver is Core::__internalObjectBase::getClassMetaSafeInternal, which
walks a function-pointer list unrolled eight at a time with a count & 7
remainder tail, dispatching every entry. It now counts what it dispatches and
flags whether igFile's address is ever among them:

    drv: n=%u sawIgFile=%u

  sawIgFile=0  -> the list the driver walks does not contain igFile, even
                  though its entry exists in .rodata. The question becomes
                  what builds that list and why this entry is not in it.
  sawIgFile>0  -> the list DOES contain it and the dispatch itself fails,
                  which would be a different bug entirely and would show up
                  in the dispatch-miss counter.

Those need different fixes, which is why it is measured rather than reasoned
about -- the same reason the probe before it separated "never entered" from
"entered and failed", and that answer is what made this question the right one
to ask next.
