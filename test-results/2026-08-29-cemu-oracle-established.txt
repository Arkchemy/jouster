WHAT DID NOT WORK, AND THE LIKELY REASON
-----------------------------------------
Breakpoints at 0x2156958 and 0x215db84 never fired, even though the game ran
far enough to reach the press-A screen and must have executed both. The log
shows CPU-Mode: 3, the JIT recompiler. Cemu's gdbstub breakpoints are
generally only reliable under the interpreter, so the next attempt should add:

    --force-interpreter

It will be much slower but breakpoints should actually hit.

Two other operational notes:
  - The stub does not implement detach ("Remote doesn't know how to detach").
    A second gdb connection after the first exits fails with
    'vMustReplyEmpty': timeout. Restart Cemu between debugging sessions.
  - The game does not start executing on its own once the stub is enabled; it
    waits. Either continue from gdb or start it in the GUI.
