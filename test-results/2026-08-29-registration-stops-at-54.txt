All three frames full through the game's own Bink API, and the watch on

consistent with everything else. The decoder is no longer between the engine
and a picture.

THE BOOT BLOCKER
----------------
    classes carrying an arkRegisterInternal: 1007
    all of them called somewhere in the tree: 1007
    reaching appendToArkCore on hardware:       54

Registration gets about 5% through and stops. That single ratio explains the
whole downstream chain that has been chased for two days:

  registry index 540 is NULL  -- of course it is; registration never got past
    54, and 540 is far beyond that
  setCapacity takes reallocateFieldMemory(obj=0)
  the list ends up with a count and no buffer
  igStringRefList::setAll walks guest addresses 0,4,8...
  garbage reaches internalRelease as a container
  igStringPool::remove spins on bucket index 0x811C9DC5

And the hang is INSIDE registration, not after it:
setMetaFieldBasicPropertiesAndValidateAll is field setup for a class being
registered. So the boot dies partway through registering roughly the 54th
class, and everything else is consequence.

This reframes two days of work. The NULL registry entry was never the root --
it was a symptom of registration stopping, and every layer chased below it was
downstream of that.

WHAT THIS BUILD MEASURES
-------------------------
A probe on appendToArkCore logging the last handful of registrations before
the stall: the sequence number, the metaobject, its type index and its name
pointer. That turns "it dies around the 54th class" into a named class, which
is the difference between a region to search and a thing to read.

CORRECTION CARRIED FORWARD
--------------------------
The earlier claim that the four recompiler fixes moved the boot twelve times
further was wrong; that rise was the recompiled Bink decoder sharing the same
call counter. The boot's real figure is 35,221 calls against 35,689 before any
of them. The fixes were worth having on their own merits -- computed jump
tables alone silently broke every switch in the game -- but they did not
advance the boot.
