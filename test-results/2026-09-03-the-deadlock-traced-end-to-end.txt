TWO OF MY HYPOTHESES DIED HERE
------------------------------
1. "igArkCore::init never runs." It runs, at call 56,621, very early.
2. "The unparsed config is the cause." It is a SYMPTOM. init descends into
   the bootstrap load and parks there, so it never reaches the alchemy.xml
   registration at 2147fc4. That is why addReadOnlyMemoryEntry fired once,
   for ram:/tfbGameIGX.igx, and never for the config.

WHERE THE GAME THREAD ACTUALLY IS
---------------------------------
    last_pc     0x21a564c = Core::igScopeLock::~igScopeLock (entry)

The game thread has been going round this loop for the entire run. That is
exactly why igArchive::updateTasks shows 56,659 hits and why calls climbs
past 3.9 million with nothing hung. The pump is working. It simply cannot
finish, because what it waits on needs a job nothing will run.

So: the game thread blocks on a file read; the read needs its block
decompressed; the decompression is queued as a background job; the only
things that could execute that job are two worker threads whose wake
condition nothing ever sets, and an inline path that is never called.

WHAT IS NOW CERTAIN
-------------------
  * igArkCore::init runs and does not return
  * the game thread is in blockUntilComplete, pumping, not hung
  * updateTasks is correct and always was
  * the decompression batch is submitted exactly once
  * nothing anywhere executes it
  * the workers cannot wake, and no instruction in the binary ever sets
    either flag that would let them (confirmed by store watch, not just by
    static search)

WHAT IS NOT YET KNOWN
---------------------
Why retail does not deadlock here. Three candidates, in order of how much I
would bet on them:

  1. The pump (vtable slot 0x13c) is supposed to execute pending jobs on the
     calling thread, and in our build it resolves to something that does not.
     Identifying what slot 0x13c is for an igArchive is the next job.
  2. The workers' wake flags are set through a store our address folding has
     mis-attributed, so the store watch is watching the wrong word.
  3. Something in the bootstrap path should have made the batch blocking
     (addBatch's r7), which would run it inline immediately.

A METHOD NOTE, WRITTEN AGAINST MYSELF
-------------------------------------
Today produced roughly six confident theories and measurement killed five of
them: nothing sets the limit, the empty list, the removal path, the missing
workers, the atomics, the unparsed config. Every one explained the evidence
available when it was formed.

The two that survived contact were the ones taken from the engine's own
words rather than inferred: the igFileWorkItem status enum from igRewrite8,
and the igArchiveBlockTask field names from the binary's own reflection
metadata. Both were decisive within minutes.

The lesson is not "theorise less". It is that a name recovered from the
artifact beats a structure inferred from its shape, every time, and should
be the first thing tried rather than the last.
