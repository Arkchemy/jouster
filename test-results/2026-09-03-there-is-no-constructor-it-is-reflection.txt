THERE IS NO HAND-WRITTEN CONSTRUCTOR
-------------------------------------
igArchiveWorkItem is never constructed by code that knows what an
igArchiveWorkItem is. It is allocated and initialised REFLECTIVELY:
getClassMetaSafeInternal lazily builds the class's metaobject, and
igMetaObject::createInstance allocates from the pool and populates the
instance from that metaobject's field descriptors.

Which means the field the archive gates on is not "a field a constructor
forgot to set". It is a reflected field whose initial value comes from
metadata. If the metadata is absent, empty, or short, createInstance has
nothing to initialise from and the field keeps whatever the pool left it --
zero.

That is exactly consistent with what the store watch recorded: three writes
during construction, the last one zero, and nothing ever again.

WHY THIS FITS THE PROJECT'S OWN HISTORY
----------------------------------------
This engine is reflective throughout, and this project has already spent
weeks on that machinery: appendToArkCore registering 965 classes,
endArkRegister, getClassMetaSafeInternal appearing in the retail-vs-ours
comparison on 2026-08-30 (retail 13,567 calls against our 105), and
blaster's extract_field_schema.py recovering 616 field names statically
because field OFFSETS need a live game.

A class can be registered -- counted in the 965 -- and still carry a field
table that is empty or wrong. Nothing measured so far distinguishes those.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT CLAIMED

That +0x18 IS a reflected field with a non-zero default. That is the
